Core Services

Five disciplines. One accountable partner.

Each service below can run standalone or as part of a single managed program — so security, compliance and infrastructure decisions never work against each other.

01 · Protect

Cyber Security

End-to-end protection covering network, endpoint and cloud environments, with monitoring and response built around your actual risk profile.

  • Threat monitoring & detection
  • Firewall, endpoint & email security
  • Incident response planning
  • Security awareness training
02 · Verify

VAPT

Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing that finds exploitable weaknesses before someone outside your organization does.

  • Network & infrastructure testing
  • Web & mobile application testing
  • Configuration & cloud reviews
  • Risk-ranked remediation report
03 · Contain

Data Loss Prevention

Policy-driven controls that stop sensitive data — financial records, personal information, IP — from leaving the organization unintentionally or maliciously.

  • Data discovery & classification
  • Endpoint, email & cloud DLP policies
  • Removable media & upload controls
  • Incident alerts & audit trails
04 · Maintain

Patch Management

Scheduled, tested patch deployment across servers, endpoints and applications — closing known vulnerabilities without breaking production systems.

  • Continuous vulnerability scanning
  • Tested, scheduled patch rollout
  • Compliance & patch-status reporting
  • Emergency out-of-band patching
05 · Distribute

IPTV & SMATV Solutions

Design, installation and support of IPTV and Satellite Master Antenna TV systems for hotels, hospitals, residential towers and commercial buildings.

  • Head-end design & installation
  • IPTV middleware & channel setup
  • SMATV distribution networks
  • Maintenance & uptime support
Inside Each Engagement

What actually happens once you sign on.

Select a service to see how ITSS scopes, delivers and reports on it.

Cyber Security

We start by mapping what you actually need to protect — systems, data, users — then layer monitoring and defenses around that, instead of selling a fixed bundle.

Network & perimeter hardening across firewalls, VPNs and Wi-Fi
Endpoint detection & response on servers and workstations
Email & phishing defense with staff awareness training
Incident response runbooks so a bad day has a clear plan

Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing

VAPT combines automated scanning with manual, human-led testing — the difference between a list of theoretical issues and proof of what's actually exploitable.

External & internal network penetration testing
Web, mobile & API application testing
Cloud configuration & access-control review
Risk-ranked report with retest after remediation

Data Loss Prevention

Before writing a single policy, we find out where sensitive data actually lives and moves — most DLP failures come from protecting the wrong thing.

Data discovery & classification across file shares, endpoints, cloud
Policy design for email, USB, cloud upload & print
Real-time alerting on policy violations
Audit trail for compliance & investigations

Patch Management

Unpatched, known vulnerabilities remain one of the most common ways organizations get breached. We manage patching as a disciplined, tested cycle — not an occasional scramble.

Continuous scanning to identify missing patches
Staged testing before production rollout
Scheduled maintenance windows to limit disruption
Compliance dashboard & patch-status reporting

IPTV & SMATV Solutions

For hotels, hospitals, towers and commercial buildings, we design and maintain the infrastructure that delivers live TV and content reliably to every room or unit.

Head-end design, sizing & installation
IPTV middleware, channel line-up & guide setup
SMATV cabling & signal distribution networks
Ongoing maintenance & rapid-response support
Engagement Process

A clear path from first call to ongoing coverage.

The same four stages apply whether you're starting with one service or the full program.

STAGE 01

Discover & Scope

We map your environment, current tools and priorities to define exactly what's in scope.

STAGE 02

Assess & Test

Scanning, testing and policy review to establish a factual baseline of risk — not assumptions.

STAGE 03

Remediate & Deploy

Fixes, patches, policies and infrastructure are implemented on a schedule that fits your operations.

STAGE 04

Monitor & Maintain

Ongoing monitoring, patch cycles and reporting keep the environment in a known-good state.
